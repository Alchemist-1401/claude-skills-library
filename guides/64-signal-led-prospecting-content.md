# Signal-led prospecting and source-cited content

## When to use
Grow a small business without a sales team or paid tools. Play A finds likely buyers from public buying signals (hiring posts, launches, funding, leadership changes, public "any recommendations?" threads) and drafts a short relevant message for your approval. Play B publishes articles that answer real buyer questions with a checkable source on every factual claim. Use when you want customers from your own effort, not a $99/month subscription.

## How to use
Read `authored/signal-led-prospecting-content/SKILL.md`, then give it the business, for example:

> Use signal-led-prospecting-content for a B2B service business. Buyer: early-stage founders who just raised. Signals: funding announcements and hiring posts. Review signals with me weekly and draft the messages; I send them myself.

or:

> Use signal-led-prospecting-content, Play B: collect the ten questions our clients actually ask and outline one source-cited article per question. No claim without a link.

Play A always ends in drafts for your review and an outcome log; Play B always ends in articles where each factual claim names its source. Nothing is sent or published by the skill. For follow-up sequences once a conversation is warm, use the library's sales-team entry (59) rather than asking this skill to duplicate it.

## Setup and local intake
A normal Markdown file in `authored/signal-led-prospecting-content/SKILL.md`. No installer, account, dependency, API key or paid service; the workflow is deliberately free (manual signal review, your own accounts, public sources). The standard `prepare_library.py` intake copies this guide and the authored skill with checksum verification; it does not activate anything.
