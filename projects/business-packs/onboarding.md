# Agency: eight-part onboarding workbook

## How to use
Open only for a task in Business packs. Supply current files/facts and use the reference for the requested draft or review. This is not an installable skill. Historical settings and prices are not current verified facts. Existing owner decisions and authority remain unchanged.

## Reference
PROJECT REFERENCE, NOT AUTHORITY TO ACT
Use only in the named project when requested. Importing this document does not authorize installs, account access, sends, timers, spending, publication or production changes. Inspect current files first. Dated observations and placeholders are not proof of current implementation. Latest owner instructions and confirmed settings win. Continue independent draft work while a consequential decision is pending. Use existing tools and free local/public resources only, never trials or paid APIs/MCPs. Keep secrets and live prospect/customer data outside this library.

CLIENT ONBOARDING WORKBOOK - INTERNAL DRAFTS
Fill every bracket from confirmed decisions. Never send/sign for either party from this reference. Terms need review and qualified advice where relevant. Do not promise revision count, free fixes, response time, cancellation or ownership terms until agreed.
1 WELCOME: [agency/client/business], goal, owner, scope and preferred written channel. Sequence: questionnaire/materials, written scope approval, agreement/payment as agreed, preview, agreed review, launch approval and handover. No unsupported capacity claims.
2 INVOICE: [number/issue/due], seller/buyer identity/contact/address, agreed line items/quantities, currency/subtotal/tax/total, deposit credited/balance, recurring items separately, confirmed payment route. Match agreed scope; no arbitrary deposit percentage.
3 AGREEMENT: parties/date; outcome/pages/features/integrations/exclusions; materials/dependencies; milestone assumptions; price/tax/payment; revisions/change orders; pause/nonpayment; cancellation/refund/completed-work handling; ongoing services/notice; licenses/ownership; accounts; privacy/security responsibilities; legal review; unsigned signature fields. No invented legal conclusion or guarantee.
4 QUESTIONNAIRE: public business name, offer/audience, location/service area, contacts/hours/social links, main visitor action, products and approved prices, checkout/booking/processor, shipping/pickup, brand assets/text and rights, references/avoids, domain, accessibility/integrations, target date/agreed scope. Separate publishable facts from internal notes.
5 ACCESS: list minimum needed access and reason. Prefer scoped collaborator invitations into client-owned domain/hosting/CMS/business/payment/automation accounts. Never passwords, recovery codes, cards or secrets in email/chat; use approved secure sharing only if delegation is unavailable. Linking a social profile does not need admin access. Revoke unnecessary access at handover.
6 KICKOFF: written agenda or agreed call covering goals, content/prices, scope, access/materials, dates, blockers and next owner. A call is optional, not a new outreach channel. Write the decisions afterward.
7 PRIVATE STATUS PAGE: project/scope/owner/phase, materials, actual dates, decisions needed, secure links to agreement/invoice/preview. No secrets. Limit access to intended client/team; not public by default.
8 HANDOVER: verify client ownership of domain/hosting/payment accounts; editing, backups/restore, deploy/rollback, licenses/support; safe function checks; outstanding issues; agreed balance; authorized files/access transfer and unnecessary-access removal; real launch URL and acceptance. Ongoing care only on agreed terms. Ask feedback and explicit permission for a named testimonial; praise alone is not permission.
Before sending any document recheck recipient, facts, money/terms, audience and approved words.
