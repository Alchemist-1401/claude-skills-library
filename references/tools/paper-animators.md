## Paper Animators

Site: https://paperanimators.com/ (studio at https://paperanimators.com/studio/)

What it does: browser-based paper-style motion graphics. Paper cutouts from photos (subject gets a moving torn-paper edge), motion templates (animated type, torn headlines, map journeys), and text match cuts (one highlighted word held in place while newspaper pages change around it), plus a background remover and a library of about 40 effects. Exports short clips for video edits.

Free scope, verified on the pricing page 19 November 2026:

- Free account: 3 exports, 720p, no watermark. Account (email signup) is required to export anything.
- Paid Unlimited: $4.99/month or $29.99/year, 1080p, all effects, unlimited exports, Stripe billing, cancel before renewal.
- The free tier is a capped free tier, not a trial that converts silently; no card is asked for on signup.

Privacy notes from the site's own pages: an analytics consent banner offers Decline; the banner states photos, videos and project text are not sent to analytics. The site runs third-party house ads (Webler). One commenter on the reel claimed a browser flagged the site; the site itself loaded cleanly over HTTPS with normal privacy/terms pages, and no independent malware evidence was found, but as with any upload-based tool, do not upload sensitive images.

Example ask after the user's own account decision:

> Use Paper Animators to make a 5-second text match cut on the word "SPEED" for the Velocitas reel, newspaper pages changing behind it. 720p free export is fine.

## Free alternatives already in this library

- Entry 60 (`editorial-texture-backgrounds`) is the underlying technique, free and tool-agnostic: dark base, rotated paper/newspaper texture, tinted-down whites, cut-outs, editorial type. It produces the same editorial look without a hosted app, inside EffectCraft, After Effects or code.
- Entries 13, 45 and 61 (EffectCraft) are the free local pipeline this library already standardizes on; reach for Paper Animators only when its ready-made templates are worth the hosted account and the 3-export cap.
