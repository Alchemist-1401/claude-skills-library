# Story rules for explainers

Own work. Adapt freely.

1. **Hook (0-2s):** a question the viewer wants answered, or a surprising number. Not "In this video".
2. **Promise:** one line on what they will understand by the end.
3. **Build:** 3 to 5 beats. Each beat answers the question the previous one raised. Alternate abstract and concrete:
   idea, picture, number, example.
4. **Turn:** one beat that corrects a common misunderstanding.
5. **Takeaway:** one sentence they can repeat to a friend. This is the outro headline.

Narration: 12 to 18 words per sentence at most, 2 sentences per beat, 35 words per beat. Read it out loud.
Use "you". Numbers: "about 40 percent", not "39.87%". Put the key word at the end of the sentence.

Pairing visual and voice: the picture shows what the voice names. If the voice says three things, show three
things. Do not show a chart the voice does not mention.

Short (9:16, 30-60s): 5 to 7 beats, hook beat max 5s, skip the quote and timeline beats, keep the outro short.
Long (16:9, 3-6 min): add a recap beat every 4 beats, and a source-heavy beat for each claim worth checking.
Cut a short from a long script with `--beats hook,stat1,outro`.
